Bio Heavy Oil Supplier & Exporter in Malaysia

SafOil supplies Bio Heavy Oil — a mixed fatty acid of vegetable origin, produced in Malaysia, that buyers use as a renewable fuel oil and as a versatile feedstock. Chemically it is a blend of saturated and unsaturated straight-chain fatty acids: a dark brown to black liquid that handles much like a conventional heavy fuel oil, but comes from renewable, plant-based sources.

A renewable fuel for heat and power

Bio Heavy Oil's main use is as a fuel. Power plants, industrial boilers and marine operators use it to replace part or all of their fossil heavy fuel oil, cutting the carbon footprint of the heat and power they produce. Because it is a fatty-acid fuel rather than a refined petroleum product, burner settings, preheating and storage should be matched to the material — our trade desk shares the full specification and handling data so your engineers can confirm fit before the first delivery.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bio Heavy Oil?

Bio Heavy Oil is a mixed fatty acid — a blend of saturated and unsaturated straight-chain fatty acids of vegetable origin from Malaysia. It is a dark brown to black liquid used mainly as a renewable fuel oil, and also as feedstock for biodiesel, oleochemicals and lubricants.

Where is Bio Heavy Oil used?

Its primary use is as a fuel in power plants, industrial boilers and marine applications. It is also suitable for biodiesel (FAME/HVO) production, oleochemicals such as soaps and fatty alcohols, energy ingredients for animal feed, and metallic soaps for lubricants and greases.

Is Bio Heavy Oil classified as dangerous goods?

No. Bio Heavy Oil is not classified as dangerous goods for sea, air or road transport, and it is readily biodegradable. A full MSDS and handling data are available on request.

How is Bio Heavy Oil packed and shipped?

We ship in flexitanks, IBC totes or steel drums, FOB Port Klang or CIF to your destination port, with a certificate of analysis for every shipment.
